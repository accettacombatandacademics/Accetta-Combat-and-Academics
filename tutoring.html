Inside the sessions

How we work

The best progress often happens between sessions, not just during them. Outside of our weekly lessons, I offer every student free support — send through tests, practice papers or exams whenever you like and I will mark them at no extra charge. Students can also reach out with questions any time by text or email, even when a lesson is not booked. No question is too small, and you never have to wait until the next session to get unstuck.
